Our Story
Born from a Real Problem

It started with a frustrating afternoon in Abuja. A burst pipe, three phone calls, two no-shows, and a flooded bathroom floor. Finding a reliable plumber — or any skilled tradesperson — felt like a lottery.

The problem wasn't that skilled people didn't exist. They did. In every street, every neighbourhood across Africa. The problem was discovery and trust. How do you find a good electrician in your area? How do you know they're reliable? How do you pay safely?

Servana was built to answer those questions. A single platform where Africa's skilled workforce can be found, trusted, booked, and paid — all in one place.
