Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Servana ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our platform, mobile application, or any related services.

We take your privacy seriously. We only collect data that is necessary to provide our services, and we never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

In simple terms: We collect what we need to run Servana, we use it to make the platform work for you, and we protect it with industry-standard security. That's it.

By using Servana, you consent to the data practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Platform.

We collect the following categories of information:

3
How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Platform — create and manage your account, process bookings, facilitate payments and payouts
  • Connect Users — match seekers with appropriate service providers based on location and category
  • Communication — send booking confirmations, status updates, and important platform notifications
  • Safety & Security — detect fraud, investigate disputes, verify provider identities, and enforce our Terms
  • Customer Support — respond to your enquiries, resolve complaints, and improve our services
  • Platform Improvement — analyse usage patterns to improve features, fix bugs, and enhance user experience
  • Legal Compliance — meet our obligations under Nigerian law and respond to lawful requests from authorities

We will never use your data for purposes that are incompatible with those listed above without first obtaining your consent.

5
Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure, which operates globally with data centres in multiple regions. Firebase uses industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256).

We implement the following security measures:

  • All passwords are hashed using Firebase Authentication — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Access to data is governed by Firestore Security Rules that enforce role-based permissions
  • Provider bank account details are stored securely and only used for payout processing
  • Push notification keys are stored server-side and never exposed in the application source code
  • Regular security reviews of platform rules and permissions
Note: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% immune to breaches. In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.
