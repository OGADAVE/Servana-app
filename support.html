📅 Bookings
Common questions about making and managing bookings
How do I book a service on Servana?

Go to Find Services from your dashboard. Browse or search for the service you need, then tap on a provider's card to view their profile. Tap Book Now, fill in your service details, date, time, and address, then confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation immediately.

Can I cancel a booking after it's been placed?

Yes, you can cancel a pending booking at any time from your Bookings page. Once a provider has accepted a booking, cancellation may be subject to our cancellation policy. To cancel, go to My Bookings → Select booking → Cancel.

What happens if a provider doesn't show up?

If a provider fails to show up without cancelling, please contact us at hello@servana.app within 24 hours. We will investigate and process a full refund where applicable. You can also raise a dispute directly from the booking details page.

How do I know when a provider has accepted my booking?

You will receive an in-app notification and an email confirmation as soon as your provider accepts the booking. You can also check the status anytime from My Bookings — it will update from Pending to Accepted.

Can I book multiple services at once?

Currently each booking is for one service with one provider at a time. You can, however, place multiple separate bookings with different providers simultaneously. We're working on bundled bookings as a future feature.

💳 Payments
Everything about paying for and receiving money on Servana
How do payments work on Servana?

Payments are processed securely through Paystack, one of Africa's most trusted payment platforms. You pay at the time of booking. The money is held securely until the job is completed, then released to the provider. Servana deducts a 5% platform fee from the provider's payout.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfers, and USSD payments through Paystack. We'll be adding more local payment options for Ghana, Kenya, and other countries soon.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are processed in the following situations:

  • Provider cancels or fails to show
  • Service was significantly different from what was described
  • A dispute is resolved in your favour by our team
To request a refund, email hello@servana.app with your booking reference. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days.

When do providers receive their payout?

Providers receive their payout (service fee minus the 5% platform fee) within 3 business days of a booking being marked as complete. The money is sent directly to the bank account saved in your Provider Earnings tab.

👤 My Account
Managing your Servana profile and settings
How do I update my profile photo?

Go to Profile → Personal Info → Profile Photo → Change Photo. Select a JPG or PNG image under 5MB. Your photo updates immediately across the entire platform.

How do I change my password?

Go to Profile → Security Tab → Change Password. Enter your new password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it. If you've forgotten your password, use the Forgot Password link on the login page.

Can I switch between Seeker and Provider mode?

Yes. From your Dashboard, tap the mode switcher to toggle between Seeker Mode (find services) and Provider Mode (offer services). Both modes are available on the same account at no extra cost.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Security → Delete My Account. Confirm the action when prompted. Note that account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Any pending bookings will be cancelled and outstanding provider payouts will be processed before deletion.

🛠️ For Providers
Offering services and growing your business on Servana
How do I register as a service provider?

During signup, select Provider as your role. After signup, go to Provider Mode → Profile Tab to complete your business profile, add your services, set your prices, and upload portfolio photos. The more complete your profile, the more bookings you receive.

How do I get verified on Servana?

Complete your provider profile fully, then tap Apply for Verification in your profile. Our team reviews your profile within 48 hours. Verified providers receive a blue checkmark badge and get up to 3× more bookings from customers who trust verified profiles.

How do I set my availability?

At the top of your Provider dashboard, you'll find the Status toggle. Switch it to Available when you're ready to accept bookings, and Offline when you're not. Customers can only see and book you when you're set to Available.

What percentage does Servana take from my earnings?

Servana charges a 5% platform fee on each completed booking. This covers payment processing, platform maintenance, and customer support. The remaining 95% is your net earnings, paid out within 3 business days of job completion.

🛡️ Safety & Trust
How we keep Servana safe for everyone
How does Servana verify providers?

Our team manually reviews provider applications. We check their profile information, portfolio, and where possible, verify their identity and credentials. Verified providers display a blue checkmark badge. All providers are rated by customers after each job.

How do I report a provider for bad conduct?

Email hello@servana.app with the booking reference and a description of the issue. We take all safety reports seriously and investigate within 24 hours. In urgent situations, please contact emergency services first.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Servana never stores your card details. All payments are processed by Paystack, which is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of payment security. Your card information goes directly to Paystack and is never seen by Servana or the service provider.
