Delete your Servana account

Applies to the Servana website and the Servana mobile app (Android and iOS).

Option 1: delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Sign in to Servana (app or website). For security, sign in again just before deleting.
  2. Open Profile (app) or Settings (website).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, email hello@servana.app from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my Servana account. We will confirm and complete the deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and your profile (name, email, phone, location, photo).
  • If you are a provider: your public listing, your services, and your bank/payout details.
  • Your pending bookings are cancelled.

What we keep, and why

  • Completed bookings and reviews stay on record for the other person involved. They contain names and booking details only, never bank details.
  • Records we must keep for fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal reasons, for as long as the law requires.
